Morning Coffee in the Shade

Find a shaded bench in Washington Square Park for your morning coffee break.

Each dot is a bench, coloured by how much shade it gets for the month and hour you pick. Set a minimum shade level and the map highlights the best bench: the shaded bench nearest to you if you share your location, or the shadiest one if you don't.

Click any bench to draw the walking route to the nearest café open at that hour.

Shade is modelled for four months (Mar / Jun / Sep / Dec) across a 7–11 a.m. window. Bench and shade data are paired. See the legend for what each colour means.
